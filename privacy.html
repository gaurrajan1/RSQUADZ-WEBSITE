RSQUADZ LLP ("RSQUADZ", "we", "us") operates rsquadz.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or submit a procurement or supplier enquiry, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, designation, work email, phone/WhatsApp).
  • Enquiry details you submit (component descriptions, quantities, delivery locations, and any files such as BOMs, drawings, or datasheets).
  • Supplier application details (company name, location, product category, website, certifications).
  • Basic technical data your browser sends (for example, IP address and device/browser type).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations, comparisons, and verification work.
  • To evaluate supplier applications against our verification criteria.
  • To communicate with you about your request and our services.
  • To meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to deliver the service you requested — for example with freight partners, verified CHA (customs) partners, or suppliers relevant to your enquiry — and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep enquiry and supplier records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
